The demo *Mito stress test* (`demos/src/mitostress/`) uses an illustrative proton
circuit and seeded measurement noise, with no external dataset. Calculations
follow the [Agilent assay guide](https://www.agilent.com/cs/library/usermanuals/public/XF_Cell_Mito_Stress_Test_Kit_User_Guide.pdf).
The clinical example of mitochondrial donation follows the
[2025 Newcastle clinical report](https://eprints.ncl.ac.uk/307272): transmission
risk is reduced, but some maternal mtDNA can be carried over.
