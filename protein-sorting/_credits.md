Cells, organelles and protein shapes in all diagrams are cartoons, not to scale. The
fluorescence panels in `fig05` are drawn after the classic SV40 NLS experiments, and the
gel in `fig12` is a schematic, not real data. Example sequences in `fig04` are from
well-studied proteins (albumin, yeast COX4, SV40 large T antigen, PKI); the
transmembrane segment is a typical pattern, not a specific protein.

external data or images.
