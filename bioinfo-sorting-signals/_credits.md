No external images are copied into this module. Plots are made by students from
UniProt data. Students view Reactome, STRING and QuickGO on the source websites.

| `data/uniprot-sequences-2026-11-18.fasta` | Original FASTA response for the same 15 accessions | UniProtKB 2026_04, retrieved 2026-11-18; exact request in `data/uniprot-provenance.json` | [CC BY 4.0](https://www.uniprot.org/help/license) |

Data providers: The UniProt Consortium; Gene Ontology Consortium and contributing
annotation groups. Browser checks also used the
[STRING human LDLR network](https://string-db.org/cgi/network?identifiers=LDLR&species=9606)
and [Reactome R-HSA-8856825](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8856825),
checked 2026-11-18. STRING associations are not interpreted as proof of direct binding.
