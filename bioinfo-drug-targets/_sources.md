## Published data snapshot

| File | Content and provenance | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `data/imatinib-ic50-2026-11-18.csv` | 158 ChEMBL 37 IC50 activity records for imatinib (CHEMBL941) against ABL1 (CHEMBL1862), KIT (CHEMBL1936), PDGFRA (CHEMBL2007) and SRC (CHEMBL267). Retrieved 2026-11-18 with the query below (originally together with EGFR, CHEMBL203, whose 3 records were dropped on 2026-11-19); columns `target`, `activity_id`, `target_chembl_id`, `standard_relation`, `standard_value`, `standard_units`, `assay_type`, `assay_description`, `document_chembl_id`. Re-fetched live on 2026-11-19: identical counts and results (158 records, ChEMBL_37). | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://chembl.gitbook.io/chembl-interface-documentation/about) |

API call used by the template:
`https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/activity.json?molecule_chembl_id=CHEMBL941&standard_type=IC50&target_chembl_id__in=CHEMBL1862,CHEMBL1936,CHEMBL2007,CHEMBL267&limit=1000&only=activity_id,target_chembl_id,standard_relation,standard_value,standard_units,assay_type,assay_description,document_chembl_id`
(release from `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/status.json`, `chembl_db_version`).

Results on the snapshot (solution, checked 2026-11-19): wild-type medians PDGFRA 71 nM,
ABL1 228.5 nM, KIT 319 nM, SRC 100 000 nM (all SRC records are ">" bounds of
5000–100 000 nM); most frequent mutation T315I (12 records), median 10 000 nM.

EGFR was dropped from the R part because ChEMBL 37 holds one exact EGFR IC50 of
0.11 nM (activity 24962906, document CHEMBL5214926) that conflicts with
>100 000 nM (activity 909964) and the known lack of EGFR inhibition by imatinib.
