predictions, not experimental data or clinical response estimates.

The *Drug journey* demo (`demos/src/drugjourney/`) is a manually stepped,
qualitative explanation. It uses no external dataset and predicts no dose,
kinetics or treatment benefit. Its baseline represents a successfully delivered,
responsive example cell. Failed ER targeting assumes no alternative functional
ER-targeting signal; translation, stability and immune responses need separate
measurements.

Primary sources supporting the examples (no figures copied):

- [Gilleron et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.2612): imaging of LNP-mediated
  **siRNA** delivery and endosomal escape. Its measured efficiencies are not
  treated as universal values for mRNA vaccination.
- [Pardi et al., 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4624045/): reporter
  expression after mRNA–LNP delivery in mice, distinguishing productive expression
  from uptake of a carrier label.
- [Druker et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11287972/): clinical responses
  to BCR–ABL kinase inhibition in CML.
- [Gorre et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11423618/): BCR–ABL mutation
  or amplification as mechanisms of imatinib resistance in the studied samples.
