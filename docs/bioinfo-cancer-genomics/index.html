Bioinformatics: BRAF V600E and vemurafenib

Follow one oncogene and its drug through four databases: the mutation in tumours, the protein, the drug bound to it, and the trial that tested it. ELO 9.

Builds on cancer, cell death beyond apoptosis, the disease casebook and the drug journey. About 2 hours.

Part A: in the browser

A1. The hotspot in melanoma

Open the BRAF and TP53 mutations in TCGA melanoma in cBioPortal. The Mutations tab opens with BRAF selected. Each lollipop is one amino-acid position; its height is the number of patients with a mutation there. Then click TP53 and compare.

T1. At which amino-acid position does BRAF have by far the tallest lollipop? [ELO 9]

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

Position 600 (valine 600, label “V600E/K/R”). Most of these are V600E. TP53, in contrast, has many short lollipops spread over the protein.

A2. Where is V600 in the protein?

Open BRAF, UniProt P15056 and go to the section Family & Domains.

T2. Which domain contains residue 600? [ELO 9]

  1. The Ras-binding domain (RBD)
  2. The phorbol-ester/DAG-type zinc finger
  3. The protein kinase domain
Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

c. The protein kinase domain covers residues 457–717. V600E sits in the activation segment of the kinase and keeps BRAF active without RAS, so the RAF–MEK–ERK pathway from the signalling lecture stays on.

A3. The drug in the binding site

Open PDB 3OG7, “B-Raf Kinase V600E oncogenic mutant in complex with PLX4032”. PLX4032 is the development name of vemurafenib. Scroll to Small Molecules.

T3. What is the three-character ligand ID of vemurafenib? [ELO 9]

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

032. The structure contains only the kinase domain, with the V600E mutation: the drug binds the ATP pocket of the mutant kinase.

A4. The trial

Open the BRIM-3 trial, NCT01006980, and look at Arms and Interventions.

T4. Which drug was vemurafenib compared with? [ELO 9]

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

Dacarbazine, a standard chemotherapy. Only patients whose melanoma carried BRAF V600E could join: the drug is aimed at the mutant protein. You meet trial design again in Kliniska prövningar och läkemedelsutveckling.

Part B: in R

Install the packages once in the console:

install.packages(c("rmarkdown", "ggplot2", "jsonlite"))

Download the template and the data snapshot. Put the template in a new folder and the CSV in a subfolder data/.

  • R Markdown template
  • Snapshot: BRAF and TP53 mutations

Data: cBioPortal, TCGA Skin Cutaneous Melanoma PanCancer Atlas (public TCGA data), retrieved 2026-11-18.

The Data chunk is complete: it reads all BRAF and TP53 mutations from the cBioPortal API into a table mutations with one row per mutation. If cBioPortal is down, it reads the snapshot instead (or set use_snapshot <- TRUE). Your job is to build the steps after it: read → filter → plot. Fill the three blanks ___ and knit.

  1. Chunk lollipop: put the column with the amino-acid position on the x axis. You should see two panels, like Part A, made from your own data.
  2. Chunk position-600: build a pipeline from the three given pieces, in the right order, joined by |>. It starts with the data, keeps only BRAF position 600, then counts each protein change. It prints a small table of changes.
  3. Chunk mutation-types: colour the bars by the column that holds the mutation type (look at the column names printed by the Data chunk, or run names(mutations)). You should see one stacked bar per gene.

T5. In your lollipop plot, compare the tallest BRAF lollipop with the tallest TP53 lollipop. [ELO 9]

  1. They are about the same height
  2. BRAF’s is about 5 times taller
  3. BRAF’s is about 30 times taller
Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

c. BRAF position 600 has 198 mutations; the tallest TP53 position (213) has 7. One position dominates BRAF; TP53 mutations are scattered.

T6. Your position_600 table: which change is the second most common at BRAF position 600? [ELO 9]

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

V600K (35 mutations, after 158 V600E). Both replace valine 600 and both activate the kinase.

T7. [VG] Your mutation-type plot: which statement fits? [ELO 9]

  1. Both genes have mostly missense mutations, so both are oncogenes
  2. TP53 has many nonsense, frameshift and splice mutations that cut the protein short; BRAF has almost only missense mutations
  3. BRAF has more nonsense mutations than TP53
Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

b. BRAF: 241 of 245 are missense. TP53: 23 nonsense, 3 frameshift and 7 splice-site mutations among 82. An oncogene needs one precise change that turns it on, so the same position is hit again and again. A tumour suppressor is turned off, and many different changes do that, including stopping the protein early. This is why a drug can block BRAF V600E, but no pill can bring back a lost p53.
