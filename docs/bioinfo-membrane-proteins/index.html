Bioinformatics: membrane proteins, tissues and drugs

Goal: read a membrane protein’s topology, see where a drug binds it, and plot in which tissues two glucose transporters are made. It builds on membrane structure, transporters and pumps and ion channels. About 2 hours.

Part A: in the browser

All tasks use the CFTR chloride channel and the drug ivacaftor, from the ion channels lecture. No accounts are needed.

A1. Count the membrane crossings

Open human CFTR in UniProt: P13569. Scroll to Subcellular location, then Topology. Each Transmembrane row is one helix that crosses the membrane (the hydropathy plot in membrane structure predicts the same helices).

T1. [ELO 2] How many transmembrane segments does CFTR have?

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

12, in two halves of six helices. Each half is followed by a nucleotide-binding domain in the cytosol.

A2. Find the drug in a structure

Open PDB 6O2P, human CFTR bound to ivacaftor. In the Small Molecules section, find ligand VX7: this is ivacaftor. Click its Ligand Interaction view (opens the Mol* viewer) to see where it sits.

T2. [ELO 2, 9] Where does ivacaftor bind?

    1. in a nucleotide-binding domain, next to ATP
    1. in the transmembrane region, where protein meets lipid
    1. on the extracellular loops
    1. in the R domain
Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

b) Ivacaftor binds inside the membrane-spanning part, at the protein–lipid interface. It is a potentiator: it holds the gate open longer. The structure was solved by cryo-electron microscopy at 3.3 Å.

A3. Read a prediction’s confidence

Open the AlphaFold model of CFTR: AF-P13569. The colours show pLDDT, the model’s confidence per residue: blue is confident, orange is very low.

T3. [ELO 2] [VG] Which part of CFTR is mostly orange?

    1. the first transmembrane domain (residues 81–365)
    1. the first nucleotide-binding domain (423–646)
    1. the regulatory R region (654–831)
    1. the second transmembrane domain (859–1155)
Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

c) The R region (mean pLDDT about 33). UniProt annotates it as “Disordered R region”: it has no single fixed fold, so AlphaFold cannot place it with confidence. Low pLDDT means low confidence, not a measured movement.

A4. Look up the common cystic fibrosis variant

Open ClinVar record 7105, CFTR p.Phe508del (F508del).

T4. [ELO 9] What is its Germline classification?

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

Pathogenic, for cystic fibrosis. F508del CFTR misfolds and is removed in the ER, so few channels reach the membrane. That is why patients get correctors (more channels arrive) together with ivacaftor (each channel opens more).

A5. Check how fat-loving the drug is

Open ivacaftor in PubChem: CID 16220172. Under Chemical and Physical Properties → Computed Properties, find XLogP3-AA. A positive value means the molecule prefers lipid to water.

T5. [ELO 2, 9] What is ivacaftor’s XLogP3-AA?

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

5.6. Strongly lipophilic, which fits a binding site inside the bilayer (T2).

Part B: in R

Install the packages once in the R console:

install.packages(c("rmarkdown", "ggplot2", "jsonlite"))

Download the R Markdown template into a new folder. Optional, for working offline: put the GTEx copy and the UniProt copy in a data subfolder and set use_snapshot <- TRUE.

Data: GTEx v8 public tissue medians and UniProtKB release 2026_04 (CC BY 4.0), retrieved 2026-11-18.

New to R Markdown? Read the five-minute introduction from day 1. Knit the template once untouched. All fetching code is given; the three blanks (___) show errors until you fill them.

B1. Where are SGLT1 and SGLT2 made?

The template fetches GTEx RNA levels (TPM) of SLC5A1 (SGLT1, gut) and SLC5A2 (SGLT2, kidney) in every tissue.

  • Blank 1: set min_tpm <- 10. The chunk then prints 9: nine tissues pass.
  • Blank 2: complete aes(): map median_tpm to x, tissue to y and gene to colour. You get one row per tissue with two coloured dots.

T6. [ELO 2, 9] In which tissue is SLC5A2 (SGLT2) highest?

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

Kidney cortex (about 36 TPM). SGLT2 takes glucose back from the urine in the proximal tubule; gliflozin drugs block it, so glucose leaves in the urine. SLC5A1 is instead highest in the small intestine (about 98 TPM).

B2. How many times do eight membrane proteins cross the membrane?

The template fetches the transmembrane segments of eight proteins from the transport lectures (pump, carriers, channels) and counts them.

  • Blank 3: map y to the column with the counts (its name is in the printed table). You get eight bars, longest at the top.

T7. [ELO 2] Which protein has exactly 10 transmembrane segments?

Answer (checked 2026-11-19)

ATP1A1, the α subunit of the Na⁺/K⁺ pump. CFTR, ABCB1 and GLUT1 (SLC2A1) have 12; both SGLTs have 14; AQP1 and KCNA1 have 6.

Hand in

  • No errors left: all three blanks filled.
  • Two plots: the tissue dot plot and the transmembrane bar chart.
  • Your answers to T1–T7.
