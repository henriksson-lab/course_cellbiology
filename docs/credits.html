Cell explorer (explore.qmd) and its slide screenshot

The electron micrograph and the organelle outlines in the cell explorer (and the screenshot img06-cell-explorer.jpg on the slide “Explore a real HeLa cell”) come from the OpenOrganelle dataset jrc_hela-2, “Interphase HeLa cell” (wild-type HeLa, ATCC CCL-2; high-pressure frozen, freeze-substituted, imaged by FIB-SEM at 4 × 4 × 5.24 nm), Janelia Research Campus. Licence CC BY 4.0, checked on 2026-11-18 on both dataset DOIs and on the AWS Open Data Registry entry (https://registry.opendata.aws/janelia-cosem).

  • EM data: FIB-SEM Technology Group, CellMap Project Team, D. Bennett, H. Hess, A. Pasolli, G. Shtengel, A. Weigel, C. S. Xu et al. Isotropic 3D electron microscopy reference data of wild-type, interphase HeLa cell (jrc_hela-2). Janelia Research Campus, 2020. https://doi.org/10.25378/janelia.13114211 (CC BY 4.0)
  • Segmentations: L. Heinrich, D. Bennett, D. Ackerman, W. Park, J. Bogovic, N. Eckstein et al. Automatic segmentation of organelles in isotropic electron microscopy data of wild-type, interphase HeLa cell (jrc_hela-2). Janelia Research Campus, 2020. https://doi.org/10.25378/janelia.13108343 (CC BY 4.0)
  • Papers: Xu C. S. et al. Nature 599, 147–151 (2021), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03992-4; Heinrich L. et al. Nature 599, 141–146 (2021), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03977-3.

Changes we made: one plane (y = 1.55 µm in the dataset coordinates, parallel to the coverslip) resampled to 12 nm pixels, contrast-adjusted, with a 0.2 µm band of damaged slices removed; outlines simplified from the machine segmentation, then checked by eye, with some predictions removed and the centriole and envelope drawn by hand (see demos/data-scripts/ in the repository).
